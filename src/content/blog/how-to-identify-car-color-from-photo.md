A photo can help you identify the **visible digital color** of a car. With an image color picker, you can select a point on a painted panel and read its HEX, RGB and HSL values. These values describe the pixel in the image; they are not the vehicle's original factory paint code.

## 1. Take a useful photograph

Choose a clean, evenly lit part of the vehicle. Soft daylight is often easier to sample than harsh direct sunlight. Avoid strong reflections, deep shadows, bright highlights and nearby objects reflected in glossy paint.

## 2. Upload the image and select the paint

Open the [car color identifier](/car-color-identifier/), upload your photo or use a supported device camera, then select a representative point on the body panel. Sample more than one area and compare the results.

## 3. Understand the color values

- **HEX** is a compact hexadecimal representation used frequently in CSS.
- **RGB** describes red, green and blue channel values.
- **HSL** represents hue, saturation and lightness.

Use the [HEX to RGB converter](/hex-to-rgb/) if you need to convert a value for another workflow.

## 4. Why the sampled result may differ

A camera changes the scene through white balance, exposure, image processing and compression. Metallic flakes and pearlescent finishes also reflect light differently depending on the viewing angle. A single sampled pixel cannot describe every visual effect in a vehicle finish.

## 5. How to find the original factory paint code

Check the vehicle's paint or color label, owner's documentation, manufacturer resources or an authorized dealer. The label location and code format vary by make, model, year and market. A VIN lookup may help a dealer find the original specification, but a camera-derived HEX value cannot replace an OEM code.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can a photo identify the exact car paint code?

No. A photo sampler reads visible pixel color. Exact refinishing requires the manufacturer's paint code and, where necessary, professional paint matching.

### Which part of the car should I sample?

Choose a broad, evenly lit painted panel away from reflections, trim, glass, dirt and obvious damage.

### Can I use a phone camera?

Yes, in a modern browser over HTTPS after granting camera permission. Uploading a photo is an alternative.

For a quick test, open the [ColorCode Identifier](/color-identifier/) and sample a photo from your own device.

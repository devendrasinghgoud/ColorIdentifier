Picking a color from an image means reading the color value stored at a selected pixel. It is useful when you want to reuse a color from a photograph, product image, illustration or vehicle picture.

## Steps to sample a color

1. Open the [image color picker](/image-color-picker/).
2. Choose an image from your device.
3. Wait for the image preview to appear.
4. Click or tap the point you want to sample.
5. Copy the HEX value or note the RGB and HSL values.

## Choose the right pixel

Avoid edges where two colors meet, transparent pixels, reflections and areas with compression artifacts. For a glossy object, take multiple samples from a consistent region and compare them.

## What does the result mean?

The result is the encoded pixel color in the image. It does not necessarily equal the physical object's original color because lighting, camera processing, display profiles and editing can change the image.

Automotive paint is more than a flat color value. The finish, lighting, viewing angle and clear coat all influence how a vehicle looks.

## Solid paint

Solid finishes generally use a uniform color appearance without metallic or pearl effect pigments. They can still look different under different lighting and camera settings.

## Metallic paint

Metallic finishes contain reflective particles that create sparkle and directional changes in appearance. A photograph may show highlights that are much lighter than the underlying base color.

## Pearlescent paint

Pearlescent finishes use special effect pigments that can shift hue or brightness depending on light and viewing angle. One RGB sample cannot represent the full effect.

## Why digital sampling has limits

A camera records reflected light and processes it into image pixels. Exposure, white balance, reflections, compression and display settings affect the result. Use a [car color identifier](/car-color-identifier/) to create a visual reference, but verify the OEM paint code before refinishing.

## Practical next steps

Photograph a clean panel in diffuse light, sample multiple points, and use the resulting digital values to compare visual references. For repair work, ask a qualified paint supplier to confirm the correct formula and any required variant.

Color contrast describes the difference in perceived luminance between foreground content and its background. Strong enough contrast helps many people read text and understand interface controls.

## Common WCAG contrast thresholds

For WCAG 2.x, the commonly used minimum ratios are 4.5:1 for normal text and 3:1 for large text at AA level. AAA thresholds are 7:1 for normal text and 4.5:1 for large text. Large text generally means at least 18 point regular or 14 point bold.

## Contrast is not the whole of accessibility

Do not communicate meaning through color alone. Use labels, icons, patterns or text as appropriate, and check focus visibility and keyboard access as well.

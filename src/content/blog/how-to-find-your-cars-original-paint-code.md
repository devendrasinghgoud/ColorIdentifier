Whether ordering touch-up paint pens or getting body panels repainted after a collision, guessing your car's color is a recipe for mismatched panels. Every production automobile leaves the assembly plant with a permanent manufacturer paint code stamp.

## Where Is the Paint Code Stamped on Your Car?

Unlike general digital color values, manufacturer paint codes specify exact chemical pigment mixtures. Here is where leading car manufacturers locate this information:

| Manufacturer | Common Stamped Locations | Example Code Format |
|---|---|---|
| Toyota / Lexus | Driver's door jamb sticker on bottom left (`C/TR: 040`) | 3 digits (e.g. `040`, `218`, `1D6`) |
| Ford / Lincoln | Driver's side B-pillar or door jamb sticker under `EXT PNT` | 2 characters (e.g. `YZ`, `D4`, `UH`) |
| BMW / Mini | Underhood strut tower or passenger door pillar | 3 digits or alphanumeric (e.g. `300`, `475`, `C10`) |
| Honda / Acura | Driver's door jamb or sill plate near latch | Letter-numbers (e.g. `NH-0`, `B-588P`) |
| GM (Chevy, GMC) | Service Parts Identification label in glove box or trunk floor | Alphanumeric (e.g. `WA8555`, `41`) |
| Tesla | Driver door jamb sticker | Alphanumeric code (e.g. `PPSW`, `PPSB`) |

## Can You Use a Smartphone Photo Instead?

While you can sample your car's visible color using our [Car Color Identifier](/car-color-identifier/) to identify digital coordinates (HEX, RGB, HSL) and explore closest matching verified codes in our automotive database, **a photo alone cannot substitute for the physical OEM stamp**.

Automotive finishes frequently incorporate:
- **Metallic Flakes:** Tiny aluminum flakes that sparkle and shift lightness depending on viewing angle (flop effect).
- **Pearl / Mica Coats:** Ceramic platelets coated with titanium dioxide that refract sunlight into subtle iridescent highlights.
- **Tinted Clear Coats:** Translucent layers that amplify chromatic depth beyond standard sRGB monitor reproduction.

## Verification Checklist

1. Locate the physical VIN / compliance sticker on your driver's door jamb.
2. Cross-reference the code with our [Car Paint Code Lookup Guide](/car-paint-color-codes/).
3. Double-check your owner documentation or contact an authorized dealer parts counter with your 17-digit VIN if the door sticker has faded or peeled.

Whether planning a full vinyl wrap or considering a factory respray, visualizing how a specific color looks on your personal car is crucial before committing thousands of dollars. While traditional desktop software like Photoshop requires complex manual masking and pen tool tracing, modern browser-based tools can achieve realistic recoloring right on your phone or laptop.

## Direct Answer: How to Recolor a Car Photo Online

1. Open the [Car Color Customizer](/car-color-customizer/).
2. Click **Upload Your Vehicle Photo** and select an exterior photo of your car (PNG or JPG).
3. Select the **Paint Body** brush tool. Adjust the brush size to fit your car's panels.
4. Paint over the painted sheet metal (doors, hood, roof, quarter panels).
5. If you accidentally paint over a window or wheel, select the **Erase Mask** tool to clean up the edge.
6. Enter any target HEX code or choose from preset colors like *Soul Red*, *Racing Blue*, or *Alpine White*.
7. Select your desired finish: **Glossy**, **Metallic**, **Matte**, or **Satin**.
8. Download your high-resolution PNG mockup.

## How Luminance-Preserving Recoloring Works

If you simply paint a flat color over a car photo, the vehicle looks like a cartoon sticker because door creases, panel seams, highlights, and ground shadows are destroyed.

Our [Car Color Customizer](/car-color-customizer/) uses **luminance-preserving colorization**:

$$\text{Output Pixel} = \text{Target Hue \& Saturation} + \text{Original Pixel Luminance}$$

This mathematical blend ensures that:
- Chrome body molding and specular highlights remain bright.
- Panel gaps and wheel arch shadows remain deep and dark.
- Sunlight reflections across curved fenders move naturally across the newly tinted surface.

## Tips for Taking the Best Photo to Recolor

- **Take the Photo on an Overcast Day:** Diffuse cloud cover prevents blinding white sun spots and deep black shadow puddles, making panel selection much easier.
- **Shoot a Three-Quarter Angle:** A 45° front three-quarter view shows the hood, grille, and entire side profile, giving you the best visual representation of the color.
- **Wash the Car First:** Mud, road salt, and water spots will carry over into the recolored rendering.
